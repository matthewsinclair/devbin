A cut can go public with no formula. Molt's 1.4.7 cut, on vendored devbin 0.1.28, confirmed `then, under this yes, after: bin/devbin formula publish`. It then ran every leg green, tagged v1.4.7, pushed and made the release object. Only after that did its after: step refuse: `packaging/homebrew/molt.rb holds 0 @@INSTALL@@, and a formula holds exactly one, the install body devbin generates`. The release stands, published, and the tap has no formula for it. The cut ends `after FAILED ... the release is published and nothing was undone`.

Molt's template has not adopted 0326's one install form. At its tag, the publish the formula runs (`install --prefix`) would also refuse, in VENDOR mode. Nothing before the tag asks whether the formula can render. release plans `formula publish` under the yes, and the formula step itself is skipped when release.formula is not declared. So a refusal in the template, which never depends on the tag, is found only once the cut is public.

The fix shape is devbin's: a pre-tag render check in release's plan. Wherever the cut will publish a formula, as the formula step under release.formula or an after: step running the formula command's publish, pre-flight renders the formula before the tag and refuses the cut when the render refuses, naming the template's refusal. Every check render makes that needs no tag runs there: the template's placeholders, install_refusal, the post-install step's place (issue I514ea74) and a placeholder left over. The rest waits for the tag: the commit, and in assets mode the sums.

Molt's own adoption (Molt I7892f34, hv's Molt decision 11, built by molt-dc) is what lets its 1.4.7 formula render at all. That publish is Molt's to make once it lands.
