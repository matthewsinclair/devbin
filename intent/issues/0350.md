## What happened

Ccusage's adoption of 0326 (reported by ccusage-dc, relayed by vc, 2026-11-13) found that T4's Rust reader, `version_line` as docs/dev-opt-brew.md prints it, fails `cargo clippy -- -D warnings` in an edition 2024 crate: Clippy 0.1.99 reports collapsible_if three times, on its nested `if let` blocks. Ccusage carries it as one let chain with the same logic.

## Why it matters

T4 says every binary of every compiled tool reads its line with this one reader, word for word, so a tool that denies Clippy's warnings cannot follow the contract as printed.

## Not yet known

Whether a let chain reads under every edition a tool may use, since let chains need edition 2024.
