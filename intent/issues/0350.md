## What happened

Ccusage's adoption of 0326 (reported by ccusage-dc, relayed by vc, 2026-11-13) found that T4's Rust reader, `version_line` as docs/dev-opt-brew.md prints it, fails `cargo clippy -- -D warnings` in an edition 2024 crate: Clippy 0.1.99 reports collapsible_if three times, on its nested `if let` blocks. Ccusage carries it as one let chain with the same logic.

## Why it matters

T4 says every binary of every compiled tool reads its line with this one reader, word for word, so a tool that denies Clippy's warnings cannot follow the contract as printed.

## Not yet known

Whether a let chain reads under every edition a tool may use, since let chains need edition 2024.

## Options, none chosen

- Print the let-chain form in the contract, and say it needs edition 2024.
- Print both forms, one per edition.

## Resolution

Fixed in one landing on main, b0f51e1c4838713adaf57cdd8cf6240f0a3b0c57 (2026-11-13 09:33Z), unit 0350-1 from the tested tip e73bafa5, in devbin 0.1.24's batch; unpushed until devbin's next cut. It was proved on its own arm under hv's rule of 2026-11-05 (red at the base's code, green at the fix) on devbin-vc's desk.

- The contract keeps one reader, word for word: docs/dev-opt-brew.md's T4 now reads the manifest as one chain of Option steps (`.and_then` to read the file, `.filter` on the tool line, `.and_then` to find the line), then a single `if let`, with the same logic as before. It needs no let chain, so it builds under every edition, and the contract says why beside it. Of the two options the issue named, neither was taken: the let-chain form would have needed edition 2024, and two printed forms would have broken "one reader".
- The edition question is answered by the arm: Clippy's collapsible_if fires on the nested form under edition 2024 alone, and the chain form is clean under 2015, 2018, 2021 and 2024.

One new arm in documentation.bats builds the reader as T4 prints it, in a one-file crate of each edition, offline and in the test's own directory, under `cargo clippy -- -D warnings`. It skips without cargo or Clippy, by rule on CI's WSL runner, which installs no Rust. It was red at the base, under edition 2024 only, and green at the fix under BSD and GNU. 0.1.24's note carries it under Fixed.
