Evidence (utilz-vc, 2026-10-31, read by devbin-vc in Utilz's tree): the cut of Utilz 2.14.8 on 2026-10-30 left `tmp/release/20261030-2059.RELEASE.errors` saying "run in flight (or aborted before sealing) -- no completed verdict; see the .out log" (84 bytes, mtime at the seal's open, 20:59Z), while `20261030-2059.RELEASE.out` runs to post-cut's last line, "post-cut: utilz 2.14.8: Menuz.app.zip attached and the tap published", last written 21:02:02Z. The cut ran its `after:` commands (tools/post-cut) to the end and ended without closing its seal, so the record of a finished release says it is still running.

Devbin's own cuts of 0.1.18 (`tmp/release/20261029-*.RELEASE.errors`) are empty files, so the path differs: Utilz's cut runs `after:` commands and the ci step, and the close is skipped on that path, or the `after:` command's exit takes the process past it.

Why it matters now: WP01's reader and the leg contract (design.md section 11) read a seal's close; a release whose seal never closed reads as in flight, so `already` never applies to it and the record is wrong for every later reader, `release status` included. It is also the class No Silent Errors forbids: a cut that reached its end and left no verdict.

Fix: lands with WP02c (the publish and `after:` fold) in cc's lane. The RELEASE seal closes with its verdict on every path a cut can end on, the `after:` commands included; an arm runs a cut with an `after:` command to the end and reads the seal's verdict and its clean `.errors`; a second arm has the `after:` command fail and reads a red verdict naming it.



Resolution (vc, 2026-11-01 15:46Z by date -u): fixed by ST0011/02b, landed at 9a0bb40f. In its own words, lib/runlog: run_gate catches INT, TERM and HUP while the gated pipeline runs, and only notes which came. Once the pipeline has ended, the run is sealed red, the signal named in its log (note_signal) and in the seal (seal_signal, which touches only a seal already red). The caller's handlers are put back with no default between, and the signal is sent to the process again, so the exit is 128 plus its number. Its arms are releasecut.bats's signal arm, red at the base, and releaseattach.bats's attach guard. The close commit cb404bdb describes this fix wrongly, so this paragraph is the record.
