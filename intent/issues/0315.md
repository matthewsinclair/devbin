The release cut's pre-flight asks `ci-state --fires` whether a push of the candidate branch starts the project's test workflow, passing the files the release commit will change (the stamp's: the version file, its sidecars, the note and its views). Under `derived:` those files are not all known at pre-flight, so the cut passes no paths (ST0011 WP02c, as ruled). The reader then answers `unknown` for a workflow whose `push` trigger has a positive `paths:` filter, and since unknown is never read as fires, such a project is refused at pre-flight, the premise named, with the three exits: fix the trigger, `release.ci.query: -`, or a bespoke query. It is never passed wrongly, but it cannot cut with its candidate leg.

Wanted: under `derived:`, pass the stamp's known files as a lower bound. A positive `paths:` filter that matches any of them fires whatever `derived:` adds, so the reader can answer `fires` for such a project. Only where none of the known files matches is the answer still `unknown`, naming the premise.
