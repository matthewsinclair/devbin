## What happened

Reported from Intent's tree on devbin 0.1.26 by intent/vc on 2026-11-17, and relayed by devbin-vc. `bin/devbin gate rust --help` ran Intent's whole Rust gate, unbooked on a shared host, until the session that typed it stopped it after two minutes. `gate` is a command Intent declares in its own bin/.devbin/config.yaml, and its options carry run: lines such as `bin/.devbin/cmd/measured --exec bin/.devbin/cut/gate rust`. 0356 made the dispatcher answer `-h` and `--help` in any position before anything runs, but only for devbin's six gate commands: gate_help_answered (lib/resolve) returns at once unless is_gate_command. A config-declared option of any other command still goes through run_option with the flag appended to its run: line as a word (lib/gatecmd's note: the dispatcher routes a config-declared option through run_option before a handler is consulted). Intent's cut/gate ignored the word and ran. Intent fixes its own side as intent#0699: its cut/gate refuses any argument after the check's name.

## Why it matters

A reader reaches for `--help` to learn what a command does before running it, so it must never run the work. Here it ran a heavy gate unbooked on a shared host. The option's summary is devbin's, read from the config, and a run: line cannot print one, so only the dispatcher can answer for it. Every project that declares a command with options is exposed, whatever its run: lines do with a word they were not written to take.

## Not yet known

- 0356 left open whether any run: line needs `--help` or `-h` as a word of its own. This is the case that question named, and here the word ran the work.
- Whether a command-level run: line, with no options, is held to the same rule. Decision 194 left it, like a project's handler, reading its own flag.

## Options, none chosen

- Widen 0356's rule past the six gate commands: for any command, where option_leaf says a declared option's run: line is what runs, answer `-h` and `--help` in any position before anything runs, with the command's usage line and the option's summary, as `help <cmd>` reads them from the config.
- Leave it to each project's run: lines to refuse words they do not take, as intent#0699 does, and say so on the pages.
