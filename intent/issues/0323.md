pplr, cldr and ccusage, three of hv's Rust CLI projects, each vendor three project commands under `bin/.devbin/cmd/` that do the same job:

- `lock-sync` updates `Cargo.lock` after the version stamp, as the release's `derived:` step (62, 62 and 60 lines).
- `macos` signs with the hardened runtime and a timestamp, verifies, notarises, and packs `<name>-<version>-<triple>.tar.gz` (249, 220 and 386 lines).
- `post-cut` is the release's one `after:` line: a worktree at the tag, a release build, `macos prepare`, `release attach --from dist/release`, then `formula publish` (94, 101 and 77 lines).

The copies have drifted: cldr-dc measured cldr against pplr at 33, 107 and 10 diff lines. A fix in one has to be made three times, eg `fact()`'s status lost under `local`, and `require_identity` reading a failed `security` call as "not in this keychain". ST0011's criterion (b) and its reach say bespoke machinery retires into the core when the core can carry it. ST0011's fix WPs name only Devbin, Utilz, Gtools, Intent, Conflab and Laksa, so these three projects are in no WP.

What the core has today: the built-in `macos` signs and notarises an `.app` (`app-sign`, `app-notarize`, `app-verify`, `publish`), as the cut's macOS stage. It does not sign or notarise a bare binary, or pack one into a release tarball. `release attach` and the formula built-in's assets mode already serve these projects' ship.

Wanted: the core carries a Rust CLI's ship as config, not code, so each project declares:

- the binaries to sign (pplr also signs a Swift sidecar and ships a prebuilt, signed `Pplr Links.app`; cldr and ccusage one binary each);
- the signing identity and the bundle identifier;
- the notary credentials, as one `notarytool` keychain profile (`--keychain-profile`), which moves in the same change (pplr vc's todo 11, hv's ruling of 2026-11-04).

`lock-sync` becomes a derived step the core runs. Sign, notarise, pack and attach become the core's own after-tag work, followed by the formula publish. The three projects delete their copies at their next vendor update.
