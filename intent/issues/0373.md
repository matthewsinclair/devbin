## What

Six consumer estates' `bin/.devbin/config.yaml` carry `log:` lines that restate what devbin does by default, so one setting is written in two places. lib/config.reference.yaml says what `log:` means on an option and on a command, and where it is read at all.

## Fix

Remove the redundant `log:` lines in the next fleet sweep. Before removing any line in an estate, check that no test or tool there reads config.yaml as text (grep its tests and tools for config.yaml), since a text reader would see the change.

Find the estates at sweep time from the trees themselves, by comparing each `log:` line with what devbin would do without it. The count of six comes from the old todo and is not re-measured.
