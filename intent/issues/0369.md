## What happened

devbin 0.1.26's pre-cut ran its bsd and bsdleg phases under a driver dc had launched with `nohup`. Both read one red, helpers.bats's arm "traps_restore puts back the handlers trap -p saved and resets every other signal named, so a caller that saved one alone gets the others back at the default (0311)" (tests/unit/helpers.bats:2558), with `HUP[trap -- '' SIGHUP]` where the arm expects `HUP[]`. lib/helpers and the arm were unchanged since 0.1.25's green cut. The same arm, run alone at the same tip in the foreground with HUP at the default, read green on Desk slot 01M7MB5A5CMYBPF5CDKBMR4VSC at 08:50Z by date -u, and 0.1.26's cut went green.

## Why

nohup starts its command, and so every descendant, with SIGHUP ignored, and a signal ignored on entry to a non-interactive bash cannot be trapped or reset there. The arm's inner bash saves the TERM handler, sets `trap '' TERM HUP USR1`, calls traps_restore with the saved line, and expects HUP and USR1 back at the default. Under nohup, `trap - HUP` cannot take, so `trap -p HUP` reads `trap -- '' SIGHUP`. The arm's comment already avoids INT for the same reason, since bash starts a background job with INT ignored, and HUP is the same case under nohup.

## Not yet known

- Whether CI or any leg will ever start the suite with HUP ignored. Every cut since 0311 landed has read the arm green, so none does today.

## Options, none chosen

- The arm reads `trap -p HUP` first in its inner bash, and leaves HUP out of what it sets and asserts when it is ignored on entry, saying so in its output.
- The arm uses a signal no launcher ignores, eg USR2, in HUP's place: traps_restore treats every signal alike, so the arm's claim stands with any two others.
- The suite refuses to start, naming the signal, when HUP, TERM or USR1 is ignored on entry, as pc0126's one-arm driver did.
